# Institution Identity and Route Selection

Keep the institution's official legal or administrative identity separate from the expert calculation route. The route is an analytical rule chosen for the available evidence; it is not an official classification.

## Why This Separation Is Required

The State Council's public-institution classification framework says classification is based on social function and must not be inferred from an institution's name, funding source, or personnel-management method. Local and central establishment authorities approve the actual classification under their respective procedures.

Official reference:

- State Council General Office, `国办发〔2011〕37号`, especially `关于事业单位分类的意见`: <https://www.ln.gov.cn/web/zwgkx/lnsrmzfgb/2011n/qk/2011n_dsqq/gwybgtwj/6997AD6DB194496C967E12D868F18743/index.shtml>
- Beijing's implementing rules, which illustrate that category recognition follows an approval process and is not inferred from the name: <https://www.beijing.gov.cn/zhengce/zfwj/zfwj/bgtwj/201905/t20190523_75447.html>

Therefore:

- do not label a unit `公益一类` or `公益二类` merely because it is a school, hospital, research institute, museum, or government-support center;
- do not call a unit `企业化管理` merely because its average pay appears high or leadership pay may raise the mean;
- do not treat `机关`, `参照公务员法管理事业单位`, `公益一类`, `公益二类`, `法定机构`, `群团机关`, `中央垂管单位`, `国有企业`, and a department's subordinate institution as interchangeable;
- write `官方机构身份未查明` when no authoritative classification source is available.

## Identity Evidence Order

Use the strongest available evidence in this order:

1. current establishment-committee approval, `三定` provisions, classification decision, or official institutional-functions page;
2. current public-institution legal-person certificate or registration annual report;
3. the unit's or supervisor's official budget/final-account organization description;
4. an official reform, audit, inspection, or government announcement that states the status;
5. official recruitment material as corroboration only.

Record the exact official wording, source, date, and whether the source is current for the report year. A historical status does not automatically survive a merger, renaming, transfer of supervision, or institutional reform.

## Two Independent Fields

Every report must contain both fields:

- `官方机构身份`: the sourced official description, or `未查明（不得推断）`;
- `作者方法分析路线`: the formula selected from the expert method, with a one-sentence evidence reason.

Examples:

- `官方机构身份：公益二类事业单位（来源：某市编办分类批复）`
- `作者方法分析路线：结构倍数路线，因为年度报告人数包含大量非编人员，无法与财政拨款工资分子匹配`

The second field may differ from the first. An officially classified public-welfare class I institution can still require the structure-only fallback when no matched denominator exists. Conversely, never claim an official public-welfare class solely because a particular formula fits.

## Route Decision Table

| Evidence condition | Expert route | Required wording |
|---|---|---|
| Local institution, matched comparable wage components and same-scope headcount | local `2/3` route | `按作者地方单位路线估算` |
| Official class-I status, few non-establishment staff supported by evidence, matched wage total and headcount | direct per-capita route | `按作者公益一类直接人均路线` |
| Mixed staffing, substantial operating activity, or no reliable denominator | structure-ratio route | `按作者结构倍数路线` |
| Source-supported leadership-skew/enterprise-style condition and matched total payroll/headcount | `1/2` route | `按作者领导层拉高均值的1/2路线` |

Do not use the last route merely because the target is a central institution, a financial regulator, or a branch system. Confirm the report scope and the route condition from current evidence.

## Special Institution Types

- **Administrative organs and `参公` units:** identify them explicitly. Their economic-classification tables can combine labor remuneration with employer-paid social insurance. Do not present the quotient as take-home pay.
- **Universities and public hospitals:** determine whether the table is only `一般公共预算财政拨款基本支出`. These institutions may also have substantial operating or service income, so a fiscal-appropriation table may not represent total compensation.
- **Departments with subordinate units:** read the compilation scope. A department report can include multiple budget units even when its title resembles the target's name.
- **Vertically managed systems and branches:** a system-wide payroll cannot be divided by a single branch's staff count.
- **Reformed or renamed bodies:** match both the entity and reporting year after mergers, transfers, or status changes.

## Compensation Meaning

Always label what the calculation represents:

- `财政工资福利支出人均口径` when based on fiscal economic-classification expenditure;
- `全口径工资总额人均` only when the source expressly covers total payroll;
- `预算估算` when using a budget;
- `作者方法下普通员工年度/月度等效估算` for the `2/3` or `1/2` result.

Do not call any of these `税后工资`, `到手工资`, or an employment offer unless an authoritative source directly supports that meaning. `301 工资福利支出` can include employer social-insurance contributions, occupational annuity, housing provident fund, and other non-cash-to-employee items.

The Ministry of Finance's 2025 central final-account explanation expressly describes government wage-welfare expenditure as labor remuneration plus employer-paid social-insurance contributions and separately lists social security and housing provident fund: <https://yss.mof.gov.cn/2025zyjs/202607/t20260715_3993597.htm>.
