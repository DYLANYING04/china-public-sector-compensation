# Code of Conduct

We want this repository to be a practical place to verify public-source research, correct mistakes, and improve coverage.

## Expected Behavior

- Discuss evidence, methods, and scope in good faith.
- Correct errors with official sources and reproducible calculations.
- Respect people and institutions. Do not use this project to target, harass, or expose private individuals.
- Keep personal data, private salary material, and unverified allegations out of issues and pull requests.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, or personal attacks.
- Posting non-public data, credentials, doxxing material, or copyrighted documents without a right to share them.
- Presenting a rumor or an expert inference as an official source fact.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile rather than opening a public issue. Maintainers may remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation to protect contributors and affected people.
